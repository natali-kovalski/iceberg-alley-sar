## Notes

- **Units.** `length` is in S2 pixels (10 m). The paper measured
  length
  and drift in EPSG:5938 metres, which are 1.070x true metres here. With that
  factor the size classes match Table 2 exactly; in true metres they are 196/175/121.
  Mean verified drift in true metres: 3692 m.
- **Verified count.** 284 paths are `manual`, the paper says 270.
  269 of them are bergs of at least 50 m; the paper says
  bergs under 50 m were interpolated (sec. 5.1), so its 270 probably counts only those.
  The scorer uses the file's `method` (250 m radius for all manual paths); ask Laust if
  the small manual ones should take the 1789 m radius.
- **Search radii** (250 m / 1789 m, sec. 3.4) were probably set in EPSG:5938 metres too,
  i.e. ~234 m / 1671 m true. Small difference; the scorer keeps the paper's values.
- **Unknown SAR objects:** 22 with a position, 47 rows
  without geometry (dropped). The paper masked bright SAR objects under clouds out of
  the scoring.
- 24 labels have area 0 and no width (14 manual, 10 interpolate): probably the bergs the paper added by hand after the S2 detection. They keep
  their `length`.
