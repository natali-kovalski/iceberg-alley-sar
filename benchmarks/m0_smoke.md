# Milestone 0c: reference CFAR detectors on one IW frame

Generated by `python -m iceberg_sar.cli bench-smoke`. Smoke test only: no labels, no
scoring. The counts show that every detector runs on our data and how far apart they are.

## Setup

- Frame: `S1A_IW_20190429T094108_DHP_RTC10_G_gpuned_671B` (HyP3 RTC, **gamma0**, HH/HV). The paper used sigma0;
  a local CFAR barely notices, since cos(incidence) is almost constant across a 520 m window.
- Multilooked 4x4 from 10 m to **40 m** (mean of linear intensity, block kept only if fully valid).
- Mask: the existing `_masked` rasters: OSM land + 500 m buffer, pack ice, nodata. Masked pixels are NaN, and pixels whose clutter ring is less than 50% valid are skipped (see `cfar_api_notes.md`).
- ENL (mean²/var, linear) in a 50x50 px open-water box at [-53.33512, 49.46904, -53.30839, 49.48757] (W, S, E, N), the median of 7081 fully valid boxes: **HH 5.964, HV 1.823**. Gamma and K use each band's own ENL (`enl_mode: per_band`, a runner option; the library itself takes one value for both). Wishart's statistic takes one ENL: **1.823** (min).
  Spread over boxes (p10 / p50 / p90): HH 3.094 / 5.965 / 8.433, HV 1.376 / 1.823 / 2.251.
- Total PFA **1e-09**; gamma, log-normal and K merge HH AND HV (per channel 3.16e-05). NIS, Wishart and iDPolRAD test one statistic at the total PFA.
- Objects: 4-connected components; library minsize 2 px, objects over 500 px dropped (paper sec. 3.3).

## Observations (2026-10-21)

- **All six detectors run on our data** through the file-based runner. No errors. The
  two library warnings are harmless here: a NumPy deprecation in K (numpy is pinned below 2),
  and the expected window crop for iDPolRAD.
- **ENL per band (decided 2026-10-22).** Gamma and K now run each band with its own ENL
  (HH 5.96, HV 1.82) instead of one shared value. With the single ENL (min, 1.82) they found
  443 and 90 objects. Per band they find 555 and 141, because HH's threshold drops from
  8.4 to 5.5 dB over the clutter mean, its correct value for ENL 6. The per-band path is a
  runner option that repeats `detector.run`'s steps. With equal ENLs it reproduces the
  library's raster exactly (`test_per_band_path_matches_library_when_enls_are_equal`).
- **Same nominal PFA, 40x spread in counts** (Wishart 32 to NIS 1295 objects). Under each
  detector's own clutter model, 1e-9 over ~18 M valid pixels means ~0.02 false-alarm pixels.
  So the counts are driven by real targets, sea ice and model mismatch, not by the PFA. Nominal
  PFA is not a common scale across detectors. That is why 2b compares at matched detection
  counts, and the paper's optimum PFAs range from 1e-2 to 1e-20 (Table 5).
- **Sea-ice strips dominate the busiest area.** The automatic zoom (densest 10 km window over
  all detectors) is a field of ice filaments in the NW of the frame, outside the 100 km²
  pack-ice mask. NIS fires all over them (136 objects in view). Gamma and iDPolRAD catch bright
  knots on the strips. K, Wishart and log-normal stay almost silent. Our CA-CFAR handles these
  with the `max_structure_m` rule, which the reference detectors have no equivalent for.
- **Gamma's 555 objects** are of the same order as the 267 same-pass IIP labels on this frame
  (Stage 1 validation, 10 m). That is only a scale check, not a score: no matching was done
  here, and the resolutions differ.
- **K and Wishart are the most conservative.** For K this fits the LUT index issue in
  `cfar_api_notes.md` (thresholds for heavier texture than estimated). Wishart's statistic
  takes one ENL, so it still runs at the low HV value (1.82) for both bands.
- The HV image shows sub-swath brightness bands (HyP3 noise-floor residuals). The 520 m
  windows are small enough to follow them, but keep it in mind for HV-driven detectors.
