## Reading

Gamma with Table 1's ENL: F 0.640 without subtraction, 0.430 with SNAP's clipped subtraction, 0.640 with the same subtraction floored at the noise level. On this scene the drop after SNAP 14 thermal noise removal comes from how the pixels below the noise floor are handled (set to ~3.7e-12, i.e. -115 dB), not from subtracting the noise. Kept negative, they break the speckle statistics outright. This is a finding about one scene and one SNAP version, not a claim of a software defect; which handling the paper's SNAP used is open (question to the authors).

Caveats: where SNAP clipped (23.6 % of HV pixels), the floor value is the smoothed noise field, not SNAP's own. On the floored stack, HV open water sits mostly at the floor, so its measured ENL is meaningless (very high); that lowers my CA-CFAR's HV threshold, which measures its ENL on the scene, and is why it does worse there than gamma with a fixed ENL.
